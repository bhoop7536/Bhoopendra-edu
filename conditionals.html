Conditionals in English Grammar

Conditionals English grammar में ऐसे वाक्य होते हैं जो किसी "शर्त" (condition) पर आधारित होते हैं। मतलब, अगर कोई चीज़ होती है, तो दूसरी चीज़ घटित होती है।

🔹 सरल शब्दों में:

Conditionals वे वाक्य होते हैं जो "अगर" (if) जैसे शब्द से शुरू होते हैं और दो हिस्सों में बँटे होते हैं:

  • Condition part (शर्त वाला भाग): जिसमें कोई स्थिति या event दी जाती है।
  • Result part (परिणाम वाला भाग): जिसमें उस स्थिति का परिणाम होता है।

🔸 उदाहरण (Examples in Hindi):

Sentence Meaning in Hindi
If it rains, we will stay home. अगर बारिश हुई, तो हम घर पर रुकेंगे।
If you study, you will pass. अगर तुम पढ़ोगे, तो तुम पास हो जाओगे।

🔹 Types of Conditionals (मुख्यतः 4 प्रकार):

Type Structure Use Hindi Explanation
Zero Conditional If + present, present Universal truth or fact अगर ऐसा होता है, तो वैसा होता है (सदैव सत्य)
First Conditional If + present, will + base verb Real future possibility अगर ऐसा होगा, तो वैसा होगा
Second Conditional If + past, would + base verb Unreal or imaginary situations अगर ऐसा होता (पर हुआ नहीं), तो वैसा होता
Third Conditional If + past perfect, would have + past participle Past regrets अगर ऐसा हुआ होता, तो वैसा हुआ होता

🔹 Examples of All Types:

Zero Conditional
If you heat water, it boils.
अगर तुम पानी गरम करोगे, तो वह उबलता है।
First Conditional
If I see him, I will tell him.
अगर मैं उसे देखूंगा, तो मैं उसे बताऊंगा।
Second Conditional
If I were rich, I would buy a car.
अगर मैं अमीर होता, तो मैं कार खरीदता।
Third Conditional
If she had studied, she would have passed.
अगर उसने पढ़ाई की होती, तो वह पास हो जाती।

🌧️ Conditionals in English Grammar

Conditionals में मुख्य रूप से ऐसे शब्द और phrases इस्तेमाल होते हैं जो किसी "शर्त" या "परिणाम" को दर्शाते हैं। ये शब्द वाक्य के condition part (शर्त) और result part (परिणाम) को जोड़ते हैं।

🔹 1. मुख्य Conditional Words (Most Common)

Word/Phrase Use in Sentence Hindi Meaning
ifIf it rains, we will cancel the trip.अगर
unlessUnless you study, you won't pass.जब तक नहीं
even ifEven if he apologizes, I won't forgive him.चाहे वो...
provided thatYou can go out provided that you finish your homework.बशर्ते कि
as long asYou can stay here as long as you behave well.जब तक कि
in caseTake an umbrella in case it rains.यदि ऐसा हो जाए तो
only ifYou can leave only if you ask permission.सिर्फ तब जब
whenWhen he comes, I will call you.जब
afterAfter she finishes dinner, she will call me.के बाद
beforeFinish your homework before you watch TV.पहले
unlessYou won't get success unless you work hard.जब तक नहीं

🔸 2. Modal Verbs used in Conditionals

Modal Example Hindi Meaning
willIf he calls, I will answer.करेगा
wouldIf I had money, I would travel.करता
canIf you try, you can win.सकता है
couldIf I were rich, I could help them.सकता था
mayIf he finishes early, he may join us.सकता है (संभवता)
mightIf I knew, I might tell you.सकता था (कम संभावना)
shouldIf you should need help, call me.यदि जरूरत हो

🔹 3. Complex Conditional Linking Words

Phrase Example Meaning
even thoughEven though it was raining, we went out.फिर भी
in the event thatIn the event that he fails, we will try again.यदि ऐसा हुआ तो
whether or notWhether or not you agree, we are going.चाहे कुछ भी हो
suppose / supposingSuppose it rains tomorrow, what will you do?मान लो
✅ BONUS Memory Tip:
जहाँ “if” है – वहाँ कोई “शर्त” है।

were

boils

stay

is

would have

rains

would

comes

will miss
15. Suppose it ______ now, what would you do?
rains
16. If he finishes early, he ______ join us.
may
17. They might come if they ______ about the meeting.
knew
18. If you should need help, just ______ me.
call
19. Even if you beg, I ______ not change my mind.
will
20. You can borrow my book provided that you ______ it carefully.
use
