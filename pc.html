Passage Completion

Passage Completion क्या होता है?

Passage Completion English grammar और comprehension का एक important topic है। इसमें एक अधूरा paragraph दिया जाता है और आपको उसमें सही sentence या word चुनकर उसे पूरा करना होता है।

Example

Ram was very hardworking. He studied day and night for his exam. ________. Finally, he secured the first position in his class.

  • A. He never cared about his studies.
  • B. His hard work finally paid off.
  • C. He stopped going to school.
  • D. He failed in the exam.

Passage Completion Tricks

  • 1. Passage का main idea समझें।
  • 2. पहले और बाद वाले sentence से logical connection देखें।
  • 3. Grammar और tense check करें।
  • 4. गलत और irrelevant options को eliminate करें।
