📘 Conjunction (संयोजक अव्यय) क्या होता है?

🔹 Definition (परिभाषा):
Conjunction वह शब्द होता है जो दो शब्दों, वाक्यों या वाक्यांशों को आपस में जोड़ने का कार्य करता है।

👉 हिंदी में इसे "संयोजक अव्यय" कहते हैं।

Conjunction Hindi Meaning
andऔर
butलेकिन
orया
soइसलिए
becauseक्योंकि
althoughहालांकि
whileजब तक कि
ifअगर
unlessजब तक नहीं
sinceचूंकि

📝 कुछ प्रमुख Conjunctions की सूची और उदाहरण:

Conjunction Hindi Meaning Example (English) उदाहरण (हिंदी)
and और She sings and dances. वह गाती है और नाचती है।
but लेकिन He is poor but honest. वह गरीब है लेकिन ईमानदार है।
or या Tea or coffee? चाय या कॉफी?
so इसलिए It was raining, so I stayed home. बारिश हो रही थी, इसलिए मैं घर पर रहा।
because क्योंकि I slept early because I was tired. मैं जल्दी सो गया क्योंकि मैं थका हुआ था।
although हालांकि Although it was cold, we went out. हालांकि ठंड थी, हम बाहर गए।
while जब तक कि She cooked while he watched TV. जब तक वह टीवी देखता रहा, उसने खाना बनाया।
if अगर If you study, you will pass. अगर तुम पढ़ाई करोगे, तो पास हो जाओगे।
unless जब तक नहीं You won’t succeed unless you work hard. जब तक तुम मेहनत नहीं करोगे, सफल नहीं होगे।
since चूंकि Since you are here, let's start. चूंकि तुम यहां हो, चलो शुरू करते हैं।

🌟 Conjunctions Quiz 🌟

1. I was tired, ___ I kept working.
Options: and / but / or / so
Answer: but
2. She must study hard, ___ she will fail.
Options: and / or / because / while
Answer: or
3. He passed the test ___ he studied well.
Options: so / although / because / but
Answer: because
4. I couldn’t go out ___ it was raining.
Options: although / because / or / so
Answer: because
5. She is very friendly ___ a bit shy.
Options: or / but / because / so
Answer: but
6. Finish your homework, ___ you can watch TV.
Options: so / and / then / because
Answer: then
7. I didn’t attend the meeting ___ I was sick.
Options: because / but / so / or
Answer: because
8. He is poor ___ honest.
Options: so / but / or / because
Answer: but
9. She will come ___ it rains.
Options: even if / or / because / and
Answer: even if
10. I will call you ___ I arrive.
Options: while / when / although / because
Answer: when
