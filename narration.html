📘 Narration (कथन शैली) क्या है?

Narration का मतलब होता है — किसी के बोले गए शब्दों को दोहराना या बताना।

जब हम किसी का कथन (बोलना) किसी तीसरे व्यक्ति को बताते हैं, तो उसे Narration कहते हैं।

🔁 Narration के 2 प्रकार होते हैं:

प्रकार नाम हिंदी अर्थ
1️⃣ Direct Speech प्रत्यक्ष कथन
2️⃣ Indirect Speech परोक्ष कथन

✅ 1. Direct Speech (सीधा कथन)

इसमें जो व्यक्ति बोलता है, उसके शब्द बिल्कुल वैसे के वैसे दोहराए जाते हैं।
Quotation marks (" ") का उपयोग किया जाता है।

🔹 Example:

Ram said, “I am hungry.”
(राम ने कहा, "मैं भूखा हूँ।")

👉 यहाँ Ram के शब्द जैसे के तैसे बोले गए हैं।

✅ 2. Indirect Speech (परोक्ष कथन)

इसमें बोले गए शब्दों को थोड़ा बदलकर दोहराया जाता है।
Quotation marks नहीं होते।

🔹 Example:

Ram said that he was hungry.
(राम ने कहा कि वह भूखा था।)

👉 इसमें Ram के शब्द सीधे नहीं हैं, बल्कि बदले हुए हैं।

📋 Direct से Indirect में बदलते समय क्या-क्या बदलता है?

🔁 Important Rules:

Rule क्या बदलता है? Example
1️⃣ Pronoun (सर्वनाम) I → he/she
2️⃣ Tense (काल) Present → Past
3️⃣ Time Words now → then, today → that day
4️⃣ Remove Quotation (" ") "..." → that

🔹 Example Conversion:
Direct: She said, “I am going to the market.”
Indirect: She said that she was going to the market.

📘 कुछ Important Tense Changes:

Direct Tense Indirect Tense
Present Simple Past Simple
Present Continuous Past Continuous
Present Perfect Past Perfect
Past Simple Past Perfect
Will Would
Can Could

🎯 BONUS TIP:

अगर Reporting Verb (जैसे – said, told) Present या Future में हो तो Tense नहीं बदलता।

Example:
He says, “I play cricket.”
➡️ He says that he plays cricket. ✅

✅ Direct & Indirect Speech: Tense-wise Formula Table

📘 Present Tenses

🔢 Tense (काल) Direct Speech Indirect Speech Formula
1️⃣ Present Simple He said, “I eat.” He said that he ate. V1 → V2
2️⃣ Present Continuous She said, “I am eating.” She said that she was eating. am/is/are + V1+ing → was/were + V1+ing
3️⃣ Present Perfect He said, “I have eaten.” He said that he had eaten. have/has + V3 → had + V3
4️⃣ Present Perfect Continuous She said, “I have been eating.” She said that she had been eating. have/has been + V1+ing → had been + V1+ing

📗 Past Tenses

🔢 Tense (काल) Direct Indirect Formula
5️⃣ Past Simple He said, “I ate.” He said that he had eaten. V2 → had + V3
6️⃣ Past Continuous She said, “I was eating.” She said that she had been eating. was/were + V1+ing → had been + V1+ing
7️⃣ Past Perfect He said, “I had eaten.” He said that he had eaten. No change
8️⃣ Past Perfect Continuous She said, “I had been eating.” She said that she had been eating. No change

📕 Future Tenses

🔢 Tense (काल) Direct Indirect Formula
9️⃣ Future Simple He said, “I will eat.” He said that he would eat. will → would
🔟 Future Continuous She said, “I will be eating.” She said that she would be eating. will be → would be
1️⃣1️⃣ Future Perfect He said, “I will have eaten.” He said that he would have eaten. will have → would have
1️⃣2️⃣ Future Perfect Continuous (Rarely used) (Generally avoided) (Convert with sense if needed)

✅ Direct to Indirect Speech – Practice Questions (20)

🔟 Present Tense Based Questions:

  1. He said, “I eat apples.”
  2. She said, “I am learning English.”
  3. They said, “We have completed our homework.”
  4. Rahul said, “I have been working here for two years.”
  5. She said, “We respect our teachers.”

🔟 Past Tense Based Questions:

  1. He said, “I went to the market.”
  2. She said, “I was cooking dinner.”
  3. Riya said, “I had lost my purse.”
  4. They said, “We had been waiting since morning.”
  5. He said, “I played football yesterday.”

🔟 Future Tense Based Questions:

  1. She said, “I will call you tomorrow.”
  2. He said, “I will be working at 5 PM.”
  3. They said, “We will have completed the task.”
  4. She said, “I will have been studying for 3 hours.”
  5. Raju said, “I will join the company next month.”

🔟 Mixed & Special Cases:

  1. He said, “I can swim.”
  2. She said, “I may go to the party.”
  3. They said, “We must finish the project.”
  4. He said, “I am not feeling well now.”
  5. She said, “These books are mine.”
