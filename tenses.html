📘 Tense (काल)

काल (Tense) वह व्याकरणिक (grammatical) रूप है जो किसी क्रिया (verb) के समय को दर्शाता है — यानी क्रिया कब हुई है: भूतकाल (Past), वर्तमानकाल (Present) या भविष्यकाल (Future)।

Tense is the grammatical form of a verb that shows the time of an action or event — whether it happened in the past, is happening in the present, or will happen in the future.

✅ पहचान की Trick (Quick Hint)
Tense पहचान शब्द
Present -ता हूँ, -ती है, रहा हूँ, चुका हूँ
Past - ता था, रही थी, चुका था
Future -गा, -गी, रहा होऊँगा, चुका होऊँगा

✅ Present Tense के चारों उपभेद (Subparts)

काल का प्रकार (Subpart) Structure (संरचना) Example (उदाहरण)
1. Simple Present (Indefinite) Subject + V1 / V1 + s/es+object. He eats an apple.
वह एक सेब खाता है।
2. Present Continuous Subject + is/am/are + V1 + ing+object. She is reading a book.
वह एक किताब पढ़ रही है।
3. Present Perfect Subject + has/have + V3+object. I have written a letter.
मैंने एक पत्र लिखा है।
4. Present Perfect Continuous Subject + has/have been + V1 + ing +sin/for + time + object. She has been working here since 2020.
वह 2020 से यहाँ काम कर रही है।

✅ Past Tense के चारों उपभेद (Subparts)

काल का प्रकार (Subpart) Structure (संरचना) Example (उदाहरण)
1. Simple Past (Indefinite) Subject + V2nd +object. He ate an apple.
वह एक सेब खाता था
2. Past Continuous Subject + was/were + V1 + ing + object She was reading a book.
वह एक किताब पढ़ रही थी
3. Past Perfect Subject + had + V3 +object. They had eaten the food .
बे खाना खा चुके थे
4. Past Perfect Continuous Subject + had been + V1 + ing + since/for + object. I had been studying for 2 hours..
मैं 2 घंटे से पढ़ाई कर रहा था।

✅ Future Tense के चारों उपभेद (Subparts)

काल का प्रकार (Subpart) Structure (संरचना) Example (उदाहरण)
1. Future Past (Indefinite) Subject + will/shall+ 1st form +object. He will eat an apple.
वह एक सेब खाता होगा
2. Future Continuous Subject + will be/shall be + V1 + ing + object She was reading a book.
वह एक किताब पढ़ रही होगी
3. Future Perfect Subject + will have/shall have + V3 +object. They had eaten the food .
बे खाना खा चुके होगे
4. Future Perfect Continuous Subject + will have been/shall have been + V1 + ing + since/for +time +object. They will have been waiting for the bus for 30 minutes.
वे 30 मिनट से बस का इंतज़ार कर रहे होंगे।

📘 Since vs For की पहचान (Pahechan in Table)

बिंदु Since For
प्रश्न पूछने का तरीका कब से? (When?) कितने समय से? (How long?)
समय का प्रकार सटीक समय (Point of Time) समय की अवधि (Duration of Time)
उपयोग जब कोई निश्चित दिन, तारीख़, या समय दिया हो जब समय की मात्रा या अवधि बतानी हो
उदाहरण समय since 2010, since Monday, since 8 AM for 2 hours, for 5 years, for a long time
वाक्य उदाहरण He has been working since 2015. She has been waiting for 3 hours.

Sub tense के चार भाग होते है जिस्मे ह्म tense के चार और भागो को दिखाते है जिस्मे हम उनके और चार भागों को दिखाते हैं।

affarmative negative introgative introgative + negative
Affirmative के अंदर हम Tense के Simple Tenses को रखते हैं Negative के अंदर नकारात्मक शब्दों को रखते हैं जो 'नहीं' या 'ना' से शुरू होते हैं" "Interrogative के अंदर हम उन वाक्यों को रखते हैं जो प्रश्न बनाते हैं जैसे क्यों, कब, कैसे, क्या आदि" "Interrogative + Negative में वे Tense रखते हैं जिनमें 'नहीं' भी आता है और प्रश्न भी बनता है"

आइए अब हम सभी कालों (Tense) के सूत्रों (Formulas) के बारे में जानते हैं कि कैसे और कहाँ क्या लगाया जाता है। तब हमें और आसान तरीके से समझ आएगा कि Tenses में कहाँ, क्या और कैसे लगाया जाता है

PRESENT INDIFINITE TENSE

📌 वाक्य प्रकार स्ट्रक्चर उदाहरण
Affirmative (सकारात्मक) Subject + V1 + s/es + Object He eats food. (वह खाना खाता है।)
Negative (नकारात्मक) Subject + do/does not + V1 + Object He does not eat food.
Interrogative (प्रश्नवाचक) Do/Does + Subject + V1 + Object + ? Does he eat food?
Interrogative + Negative Do/Does + Subject + not + V1 + Object + ? Does he not eat food?

PRESENT CONTINOUS

📌 वाक्य प्रकार स्ट्रक्चर उदाहरण
Affirmative (सकारात्मक) Subject + is/am/are + V1 + ing + Object She is dancing. (वह नाच रही है।)
Negative (नकारात्मक) Subject + is/am/are + not + V1 + ing + Object She is not dancing.
Interrogative (प्रश्नवाचक) Is/Am/Are + Subject + V1 + ing + Object + ? Is she dancing?
Interrogative + Negative Is/Am/Are + Subject + not + V1 + ing + Object + ? Is she not dancing?

present perfect tense

वाक्य प्रकार (Type) Structure (रचना) Example (वाक्य) हिंदी अनुवाद
✅ Affirmative (सकारात्मक) Subject + has/have + V3 He has eaten lunch. उसने दोपहर का खाना खा लिया है।
❌ Negative (नकारात्मक) Subject + has/have + not + V3 She has not finished homework. उसने होमवर्क पूरा नहीं किया है।
❓ Interrogative (प्रश्नवाचक) Has/Have + Subject + V3? Have they arrived? क्या वे आ चुके हैं?
❓❌ Interrogative + Negative (प्रश्नवाचक + नकारात्मक) Has/Have + Subject + not + V3? Has he not gone yet? क्या वह अभी तक नहीं गया है?

📝 Notes:

  • He, She, It, कोई नाम (जैसे: Ravi, Neha) = has
  • I, You, We, They = have
  • Verb हमेशा V3 (past participle) form में होगी (जैसे: eaten, gone, written आदि)

PRESENT PERFECT CONTINOUS TENSE

वाक्य प्रकार (Type) Structure (रचना) Example (वाक्य) हिंदी अनुवाद
✅ Affirmative (सकारात्मक) Subject + has/have been + V1 + ing + Object + since/for + time He has been reading a book for two hours. वह दो घंटे से एक किताब पढ़ रहा है।
❌ Negative (नकारात्मक) Subject + has/have not been + V1 + ing + Object + since/for + time She has not been working since morning. वह सुबह से काम नहीं कर रही है।
❓ Interrogative (प्रश्नवाचक) Has/Have + Subject + been + V1 + ing + Object + since/for + time? Have you been studying since 5 AM? क्या तुम सुबह 5 बजे से पढ़ाई कर रहे हो?
❓❌ Interrogative + Negative (प्रश्नवाचक + नकारात्मक) Has/Have + Subject + not been + V1 + ing + Object + since/for + time? Has he not been coming to class for a week? क्या वह एक हफ्ते से कक्षा में नहीं आ रहा है?

📝 Notes (टिप्पणियाँ):

  • Has = He, She, It, कोई एक नाम (जैसे Ravi, Neha)
  • Have = I, You, We, They
  • इसमें been हमेशा आता है
  • क्रिया (Verb) में V1 + ing का प्रयोग होता है

PAST TENSE के SUB PART

Tense Type Structure Example हिंदी अनुवाद
Past Indefinite
(Simple Past)		 ✅ Affirmative Subject + V2 + Object He played football. उसने फुटबॉल खेला।
❌ Negative Subject + did not + V1 + Object He did not play football. उसने फुटबॉल नहीं खेला।
❓ Interrogative Did + Subject + V1 + Object? Did he play football? क्या उसने फुटबॉल खेला?
❓❌ Interrogative + Negative Did + Subject + not + V1 + Object? Did he not play football? क्या उसने फुटबॉल नहीं खेला?
Past Continuous ✅ Affirmative Subject + was/were + V1 + ing + Object He was playing football. वह फुटबॉल खेल रहा था।
❌ Negative Subject + was/were + not + V1 + ing + Object He was not playing football. वह फुटबॉल नहीं खेल रहा था।
❓ Interrogative Was/Were + Subject + V1 + ing + Object? Was he playing football? क्या वह फुटबॉल खेल रहा था?
❓❌ Interrogative + Negative Was/Were + Subject + not + V1 + ing + Object? Was he not playing football? क्या वह फुटबॉल नहीं खेल रहा था?
Past Perfect ✅ Affirmative Subject + had + V3 + Object He had played football. उसने फुटबॉल खेल लिया था।
❌ Negative Subject + had not + V3 + Object He had not played football. उसने फुटबॉल नहीं खेला था।
❓ Interrogative Had + Subject + V3 + Object? Had he played football? क्या उसने फुटबॉल खेल लिया था?
❓❌ Interrogative + Negative Had + Subject + not + V3 + Object? Had he not played football? क्या उसने फुटबॉल नहीं खेला था?
Past Perfect Continuous ✅ Affirmative Subject + had been + V1 + ing + Object + since/for + time He had been playing football for an hour. वह एक घंटे से फुटबॉल खेल रहा था।
❌ Negative Subject + had not been + V1 + ing + Object + since/for + time He had not been playing football since morning. वह सुबह से फुटबॉल नहीं खेल रहा था।
❓ Interrogative Had + Subject + been + V1 + ing + Object + since/for + time? Had he been playing football since morning? क्या वह सुबह से फुटबॉल खेल रहा था?
❓❌ Interrogative + Negative Had + Subject + not been + V1 + ing + Object + since/for + time? Had he not been playing football for two hours? क्या वह दो घंटे से फुटबॉल नहीं खेल रहा था?

FUTURE TENSE के SUB PART

Tense Type Structure Example हिंदी अनुवाद
Future Indefinite
(साधारण भविष्य काल)		 ✅ Affirmative Subject + will + V1 + Object He will eat food. वह खाना खाएगा।
❌ Negative Subject + will not + V1 + Object He will not eat food. वह खाना नहीं खाएगा।
❓ Interrogative Will + Subject + V1 + Object? Will he eat food? क्या वह खाना खाएगा?
❓❌ Interrogative + Negative Will + Subject + not + V1 + Object? Will he not eat food? क्या वह खाना नहीं खाएगा?
Future Continuous
(चालू भविष्य काल)		 ✅ Affirmative Subject + will be + V1 + ing + Object She will be reading a book. वह एक किताब पढ़ रही होगी।
❌ Negative Subject + will not be + V1 + ing + Object She will not be reading a book. वह एक किताब नहीं पढ़ रही होगी।
❓ Interrogative Will + Subject + be + V1 + ing + Object? Will she be reading a book? क्या वह एक किताब पढ़ रही होगी?
❓❌ Interrogative + Negative Will + Subject + not be + V1 + ing + Object? Will she not be reading a book? क्या वह एक किताब नहीं पढ़ रही होगी?
Future Perfect
(पूर्ण भविष्य काल)		 ✅ Affirmative Subject + will have + V3 + Object They will have finished the work. वे काम पूरा कर चुके होंगे।
❌ Negative Subject + will not have + V3 + Object They will not have finished the work. वे काम पूरा नहीं कर चुके होंगे।
❓ Interrogative Will + Subject + have + V3 + Object? Will they have finished the work? क्या वे काम पूरा कर चुके होंगे?
❓❌ Interrogative + Negative Will + Subject + not have + V3 + Object? Will they not have finished the work? क्या वे काम पूरा नहीं कर चुके होंगे?
Future Perfect Continuous
(परिपूर्ण चालू भविष्य काल)		 ✅ Affirmative Subject + will have been + V1 + ing + Object + since/for + time He will have been working for 5 hours. वह पाँच घंटे से काम कर रहा होगा।
❌ Negative Subject + will not have been + V1 + ing + Object + since/for + time He will not have been working for 5 hours. वह पाँच घंटे से काम नहीं कर रहा होगा।
❓ Interrogative Will + Subject + have been + V1 + ing + Object + since/for + time? Will he have been working for 5 hours? क्या वह पाँच घंटे से काम कर रहा होगा?
❓❌ Interrogative + Negative Will + Subject + not have been + V1 + ing + Object + since/for + time? Will he not have been working for 5 hours? क्या वह पाँच घंटे से काम नहीं कर रहा होगा?
