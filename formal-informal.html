Formal और Informal English 1. Formal English (औपचारिक) इस्तेमाल: Official letters, नौकरी इंटरव्यू, presentation, business emails, reports भाषा: Polite, professional, respectful Example: Formal: “Could you please help me with this task?” Formal: “I would like to inform you that the meeting has been postponed.” 2. Informal English (अनौपचारिक / Casual) इस्तेमाल: Friends, family, chatting, social media भाषा: Simple, friendly, shortcuts या slang भी चलती है Example: Informal: “Hey, can you help me with this?” Informal: “The meeting’s off, just letting you know.” 📊 Difference (Formal vs Informal) Formal English Informal English I am writing to inform you… Just to let you know… Could you please… Can you… I would like to… I want to… Thank you for your assistance.
