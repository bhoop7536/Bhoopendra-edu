Urbanization has contributed significantly to economic growth by creating opportunities for industries, businesses, and innovation. Passage 7

Urbanization is one of the most significant transformations of the modern world. Over the past few decades, millions of people have migrated from rural areas to cities in search of better employment, education, and healthcare facilities. Cities are often seen as centers of economic growth and innovation. They provide access to industries, corporate offices, and advanced infrastructure. As a result, urban areas contribute significantly to a nation’s GDP. However, rapid urbanization also creates several challenges. The increasing population in cities leads to overcrowding, traffic congestion, and pressure on housing facilities. Many migrants are forced to live in slums due to the high cost of living. Environmental problems such as air and water pollution also intensify as industries and vehicles increase. Moreover, the gap between the rich and the poor becomes more visible in urban settings. To manage these issues, governments are focusing on sustainable urban planning. Smart city projects, improved public transportation, and affordable housing schemes are being introduced to ensure balanced development. Proper planning and community participation can help cities grow without compromising environmental and social well-being.
