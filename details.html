While this digital revolution has increased efficiency and connectivity, it has also created new challenges related to privacy, misinformation, and social isolation. Many experts argue that excessive dependence on technology may weaken critical thinking skills, as individuals often rely on quick online searches rather than deep analysis. However, others believe that technology, when used responsibly, can enhance learning, promote innovation, and democratize knowledge by making educational resources available to a wider population. Passage 5

In modern societies, cities are expanding at an unprecedented rate as people migrate from rural areas in search of better employment, education, and healthcare. Urbanization has contributed significantly to economic growth by creating opportunities for industries, businesses, and innovation. Well-planned cities can provide efficient transport systems, better infrastructure, and improved living standards. However, rapid and unplanned urban growth often leads to overcrowding, pollution, housing shortages, and on public services. Slums and informal settlements grow when affordable housing is unavailable, increasing inequality and social tensions. Environmental degradation also becomes a serious concern as green spaces shrink and waste management systems struggle to cope with growing populations. Therefore, sustainable urban planning, investment in public infrastructure, and inclusive housing policies are essential to ensure that urbanization benefits society as a whole rather than creating long-term social and environmental problems.

Questions:

  2. Why do people move to cities?
1️⃣Causes and effects of urbanization
2️⃣For better jobs, education, and healthcare
