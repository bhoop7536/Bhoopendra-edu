Democracy is not merely a form of government; it is primarily a mode of associated living, of conjoint communicated experience. It emphasizes participation, equality, and responsibility among citizens.

Questions:
  1. According to the passage, democracy is primarily what?
  2. Name two values emphasized in democracy.
